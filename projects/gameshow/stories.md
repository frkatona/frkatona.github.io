## Bank A

[Open Bank A](bank-a.html)

- own-goal
	- played soccer as a child (~10)
		- team name: The Titans
	- scored an own-goal that lost my team a big game
	- canceled the usual pizza party afterwards because of me
- cookie dough
	- made raw cookie dough for school event
		- fundraiser during lunch
	- another student pointed out early that this was dangerous if I didn't make it special, but I didn't like them so I didn't believe them
	- it ended up giving a dozen people and my favorite teacher food poisoning
	- had to go to the principal's office, who made me feel like he was going to call the cops
- crime doesn't pay
	- in line for lunch in elementary school cafeteria one day, I saw my friend pocket a rice krispies treats
	- I figured they were free and he just didn't have room on his lunch tray
	- I stole rice krispies treats almost every day for the next month
	- until I was caught one day
		- and the principal threatened to send me to jail and I cried
- wrong car
	- was helping my mom load groceries into the car when I was about 11
		- she had gone back inside because we'd forgotten the milk
	- put all our bags in the open trunk of a silver minivan
	- a man came over and handed me five dollars
		- thought I was one of the store's baggers
		- I was too excited about the tip to question it
	- waved as he drove away with our groceries
	- my mom came back and pointed at our silver minivan, two spaces over
		- had to explain that I'd basically sold a week's groceries for five dollars

## Bank B

[Open Bank B](bank-b.html)

- scar
	- bit by a shark at the beach
		- went with my family as a kid
		- at New Smyrna, Florida "the shark bite capital of the world"
	- wound re-opened in class without me noticing
		- I got blood on my crush
		- teacher screamed
	- it's mostly healed (show any real scar)
- not candy
	- sibling swatted a fly and it fell next to me
	- was eating m&ms at the time, so I palmed one, pretended to pick up the the fly, and then popped the candy in my mouth
		- they were grossed out, but I took a serious tone and said "it's the best protein you can get"
	- later, saw sibling swat another fly and eat it before I could stop them
- picture day
	- got gum stuck in my hair the night before school picture day
		- was trying to blow a bubble bigger than my face
	- cut it out with the kitchen scissors instead of asking for help
	- kept cutting the other side to make it even
		- eventually had almost no bangs left
	- told my dad the barber had done it that afternoon
	- he took me back to the barber to complain, with me sitting right there
		- barber pointed out that he'd been closed that day
	- had to confess in the chair, then go to picture day with my "repaired" haircut
- the audition
	- saw a line outside the school auditorium and joined because someone said there was free pizza
		- didn't realize the pizza was for kids auditioning for the spring musical
	- by the time I figured it out, I'd already written my name on the sheet
	- panicked when they called me up and sang "happy birthday"
		- couldn't think of any other song I knew all the words to
	- got cast as a tree with one line: "winter is coming"
	- my mom invited the whole family to opening night
		- they sat through 40 minutes waiting for my line
		- I sneezed right as I was supposed to say it, and the kid playing the other tree said it for me
